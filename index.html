Malaysia’s Uneven Recovery

Income rose nationally, but poverty and vulnerability remained concentrated across states, 2007–2022.

Income values are nominal RM per month and are not adjusted for inflation.

Section 1

National progress

Malaysia recorded long-term income growth and a sharp national decline in absolute poverty. The state-level view shows why national progress should still be read alongside regional differences.

Section 2

The 2022 geography

The maps compare income, poverty and the combined vulnerability pattern. They show broad geography, while the ranked charts below provide more exact comparison for small territories.

Ranking the state gap

Ranking the states clarifies the scale of difference that maps can make difficult to judge. The income and poverty rankings also provide context for the mean-median income gap.

Section 4

Recovery and persistence

The relationship between income and poverty remains visible in 2022. Time-series views show whether recovery narrowed the gap or left persistent pressure in the same states.

Section 5

Where vulnerability remains highest

The final ranking brings the income and poverty indicators together. Higher scores indicate states where lower income and higher poverty pressures overlap.
