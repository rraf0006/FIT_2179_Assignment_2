FIT2179 Data Visualisation 2

Malaysia’s income rose, but recovery has been uneven across states

Household income and poverty across Malaysian states, 2007–2022, with a 1970–2022 national context.

Income values are nominal RM per month and are not adjusted for inflation.

1. Long-term progress: The national picture

Malaysia’s long-term story is one of rising household income and a sharp fall in absolute poverty. But national averages hide differences between states.

2. The 2022 map: Income and poverty do not fall evenly

The maps show where income is highest and where poverty remains concentrated.

3. Ranking the gap between states

Maps show geography. Ranked charts make exact comparison easier.

4. Income, poverty and recovery

The relationship between income and poverty is clear, but some states remain more vulnerable than others even after recovery.

5. Final scorecard: Where vulnerability remains highest

The scorecard combines low median income, absolute poverty and relative poverty to summarise which states remain most vulnerable in 2022.

Sources and notes
